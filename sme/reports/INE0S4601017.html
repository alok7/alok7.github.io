1. Executive Verdict

SJP supplies plastic-joining machinery, custom industrial automation and laser solutions—not medical ultrasound. It has application-engineering know-how and growing recent automation orders, but the decisive weakness is cash realization: FY 2024–26 ₹12.8171 crore restated PAT generated negative ₹5.4261 crore CFO. Five-year revenue CAGR is only 5.74%, not the superficially attractive 32.36% three-year recovery. Fresh IPO capital can support the machinery/WC expansion, but it is committed capital, not free surplus. At the dated secondary ₹96.22 reference, the base model returns about 7.6%, below the 15% hurdle; IAS remains NS because executable liquidity is unverified. This is a mixed-quality capital-expenditure execution watchlist, not an evidence-backed compounder. Collections, supplier-advance conversion and independently verified commissioning—not a higher quote—would improve the thesis. [S01;S03;S04;S06;C01;A01]

4. Business Overview

SJP designs/integrates custom plastic joining, industrial automation and laser machinery, tooling and service solutions. Its B2B clients are principally automotive manufacturers, with applications in medical, electronics, textiles, packaging and others. These application lists do not establish balanced end-market revenue. Vasai manufacturing and regional demonstration/service presence enable design, delivery, installation and support. Imported machine technology combines with local tools and integration. Revenue is collected through customer orders/advances and subsequent receivables, exposing the supplier to OEM credit terms. Purchase orders, rather than long-term offtake contracts, dominate. [S01 pp 125–149]

FY 2026 domestic sales ₹22.7802 crore, SEZ ₹.1078 crore and other exports ₹3.6697 crore total ₹26.5577 crore. Manufacturing/sales/service mix changed substantially; no clean same-product ASP series exists. Reported segment and machine-count bridges below are preferable to claiming all growth is volume. [S01 p 211]

5. Moat / Industry Position

Custom application knowledge, qualified fixtures, integration and installed-process familiarity create switching friction. They do not demonstrate industry-wide pricing power, patented monopoly or dominant share. Supplier concentration worsened: top 10/top 5 purchases 88.64%/77.18%, versus 58.76%/40.66%. No binding long-term supplier contracts eliminate supply/FX risk. [S01 pp 20–22]

11. Bear Case & Permanent-Loss Path

The key thesis-break variable is collection and conversion of working capital into cash. Customer loss can reduce utilization/margins while existing inventory and advances remain stranded; debt maturity then consumes the cushion, forcing capital raising or contraction just as IPO machinery needs utilization. Aged balances and concentrated suppliers make this linked path credible. Deteriorating operational earning power and dilution are permanent-loss mechanisms; a circuit-bound quoted drawdown alone is not. Impairment risk High, not a proven current distress event. Counterevidence: real recurring profits, improving headline receivables inFY 2026, netIPO cash and low historical net-debt/EBITDA permit the core bear to fund itself in the proforma model. Those facts prevent a blanket default/fraud narrative. [S01;A01]

The financial-reliability question is proportionate: asset-label/old-account discrepancies, unconfirmed balances and mixed source tables require monitoring, but small bounded presentation differences do not alone establish earnings falsification. Evidence of disputed unrecoverable assets or material adverse audit changes would alterG2; explicit diversion would alterG1. Actual unfinanceable obligations would alterG3. Conditional model failure alone does none of those.

12. 5× / 10× Feasibility

Unsupported as a central investment thesis. Five-year 5 x/10 x price outcomes require 37.97%/58.49% annual returns. With unchanged share count and 20 x exitP/E,₹481.10/₹962.20 target prices require ₹31.1272/₹62.2543 crorePAT; at 20% netmargin that means ₹155.636/₹311.271 croresales, around 42.4%/63.6% five-year revenueCAGR fromFY 2026. Growth of that scale would need substantially more working capital and operating capability than the present evidence establishes; equity issuance raises the requirement further. These are algebraic feasibility hurdles, not forecasts or probabilities. Lower 10 xexitP/E doubles required earnings again. Even the modeled bull does not establish 5 x. [C01;A01]
