4. Business Overview

Siddhika sells and applies architectural textured/functional coatings, chiefly SK Kaken products, and undertakes contracting/engineering services. It is a skilled execution/channel business rather than the owner of a paint-manufacturing platform. Two effectively wholly owned subsidiaries, AB Coatings (100%) and SCL Contracts (99.999%), support the group. FY26 subsidiary revenue was ₹2.835 crore and ₹2.433 crore respectively before consolidation eliminations; neither should be added to consolidated revenue. [B01 pp 26,94-95; B101,B104]

The defensibility mechanism is application quality, local site coordination and a trusted route from architects/specifiers to execution. The prospectus describes SKK specifications in architects' bills of quantities, SKK marketing referrals, and Siddhika bidding/executing the supply-and-apply contract. This offers customer access without owning a mass consumer brand. It does not establish exclusivity, market leadership, or pricing power. Management's “largest single brand paint contract company” description is not independently verified market share. [B02 pp 110-115; P103]

The decisive contrary evidence is dependence on the supplier. The 2021 prospectus explicitly disclosed no firm long-term supply commitment, with authorized-dealer understanding governing the relationship. SKK represented 85.94% of H1 FY21 purchase volumes and top-ten customers 45.02% of H1 FY21 revenue. These are historical, not current percentages. FY26 continued SKK references corroborate relationship continuity but do not prove current enforceable supply rights. Current concentration and contract renewal/termination provisions are ND. [B02 pp 30,203; B01 p 36; P101,P102]

The staff base (98 permanent employees at March 2026) and applicator network permit expansion with limited fixed plant, but not without incremental supervisors, applicators, materials, retention balances and performance security. Site quality failures, labour inflation, SGD import prices, customs duties, supplier withdrawal and slow collections are linked risks. The historical prospectus notes lower monsoon activity; smooth monthly sales are therefore a modeling approximation, not disclosed seasonality. [B01 pp 1,19,32,35-36; B02 p 202]

Audited revenue composition: FY26 painting/coating contracts ₹51.2167 cr, paint/allied sales ₹4.1839 cr and contracting/engineering ₹2.5311 cr. Painting is 88.41% of total, trading 7.22%, engineering 4.37%; subsidiary revenue is not added again. Contract billing and collection include customer advances, retention and BG collateral. Geography is India; no invented region-level revenue shares. [FY26 annual report, note 20 p 86; F01]
