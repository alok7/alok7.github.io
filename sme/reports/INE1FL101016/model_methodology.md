# Model: cash-funded commodity growth, not an FMCG rerating promise

## Operating assumptions and why this is not a factory capacity model

Most revenue is commodity sourcing, cleaning, processing and distribution. There is no verified, economically comparable total-company revenue-capacity constraint, and the model does not impose a spinning-mill-like throughput limit. Its generic capacity field is a nonbinding numerical ceiling, not a company disclosure. Operating lease rents remain operating expenses; no lease debt or rent-addback is invented under this AS presentation. Organic nominal run-rate growth of 0%, 10% and 18% for bear/base/bull is an analyst range. It is not evidence of physical volume growth, share gain or branded pricing power. Rupee revenue cannot separate volume from commodity-price effects without a product/quantity bridge.

Core EBITDA margins are 2.5%, 3.5% and 4.5%. FY2026 operating EBITDA excluding all other income is approximately ₹28.1850 crore, or 3.68% of revenue, versus CARE's differently defined PBILDT ₹31.58 crore. The base does not capitalize all other income as recurring operating margin. Available cash earns an explicit 4% annualized yield; blocked/earmarked funds earn no distributable return. Forecast 92.5% material intensity is a rounded cost-of-goods-sold proxy; a 3% unpassed input shock reduces EBITDA by approximately 2.775 percentage points before other effects. A 1% sales fixed-cost base scales with annual sales (not an invented large operating-leverage advantage).

Demand run rate increases continuously: `FY2026 revenue × (1 + nominal growth)^(month/12)`. Revenue recognized in each month is one-twelfth of that run rate under the neutral 50/50 half-year split. This is an assumption, not verified monthly seasonality. As a result, year-five actual recognized revenue CAGR is lower than the run-rate growth input; both are retained. Customer-loss shocks reduce revenue from the first month and interact with collections and inventory, rather than merely subtracting terminal EPS.

Base receivables/inventory/payable days are 40/51/30, near FY2026 closing-balance proxies rather than historical-average-day ratios. Inventory and payables use model material cost; receivables use revenue. Opening stock balances transition over 12 months to those targets. Supplier advances and GST recoverable balances remain proportional to revenue; customer advances do likewise. Collections and supplier payments are nonnegative; uncollectible receivables or inventory write-offs remove assets and hit EBIT without creating cash. A 20/25/20-day improved-conversion reference is materially optimistic relative to the March balance sheet and stays labeled conditional.

## Capital expenditure and depreciation

Maintenance is the larger of 0.3% of sales and 4% annualized of modeled PPE. Neither maintenance equals historical depreciation nor is a disclosure. Additional fixed investment is 2% of each positive increase in annual revenue run rate; it is paid immediately and enters PPE, while working-capital funding is separately calculated. A 5% incremental-fixed-capital sensitivity tests underinvestment. These ratios are analytical for a distribution-heavy business, not a disclosed expansion cost. The separate historic project-spending bound is paid over 12 months, with 3/12/24-month timing and cost-overrun sensitivities.

Existing annual depreciation is ₹1.125436386 crore; modeled new assets depreciate at 8%. Depreciation cannot exceed modeled assets. Project uplift cases earn 0–1 percentage point additional margin only from the assumed commissioning month, not before; none is booked as a management guarantee. No extra project sales are added outside the single revenue growth path, avoiding double counting. The main case assumes no project margin uplift.

## Cash sources, debt and claims

Every monthly row carries opening and closing cash, liquid and restricted buckets, receivables, inventories, payables, other operating assets, customer advances, PPE, principal by loan, new-credit vintages and share counts. Available cash is distinct from earmarked IPO money and excluded deposits. The optional project bucket only releases cash in step with defined project expenditure. It does not finance dividends or count again at terminal equity.

Opening debt reconciles ₹18.200080508 crore CC + ₹21.185348053 crore FD-backed OD + ₹3.1678329 crore secured term debt + ₹2.9168145 crore related-party debt = ₹45.470075961 crore. Six term loans use their disclosed March current maturities over the first 12 modeled months and spread the remaining balance to the latest 30 May 2030 maturity boundary (44 modeled months). Those monthly allocations are **A**, not disclosed schedules. Earlier prospectus sanction/schedule inconsistencies are not silently resolved in favor of an easier debt profile. Loan interest is assumed at 9.5%, CC 10%, OD 8.5% and related-party 9%, with rate sensitivities; historical prospectus rates are not claimed current. FD OD is tested for repayment after a year and immediate repayment for release of collateral. Main CC renewal is a conditional analytical assumption; a nonrenewal stress repays it at month 12. Related-party debt is not legally represented as subordinated despite a legacy generic internal identifier; a month-one repayment stress is retained.

No new borrowing or equity finances a main case. A hypothetical enhanced-limit variant permits at most ₹25 crore outstanding, ₹50 crore cumulative draws, 11.5% interest, 1% fee and 36-month bullet maturity per individual draw. CARE's ₹45 crore rated WC figure is **not** proof of that availability. Draw-period interest and its actual tax shield are solved together; no unused-limit cash is counted in advance. New credit cannot be automatically rolled beyond the explicit five/ten-year forecast horizon, and every draw's repayment falls inside the retained transition. A separate hypothetical equity issue of 3,000,000 shares at ₹150 raises ₹45 crore gross less a 3% fee, with full contemporaneous dilution. It is not an announced capital raising. No actual options or warrants are invented.

Opening nontrade claims ₹7.418583202 crore include prior-period tax and other provisions/current liabilities excluding customer advances. These are paid separately over 12 months. Future earnings pay 26% tax only when taxable, with no assumed refund from losses. Advance tax/TDS, tax-appeal deposit and a named-person balance are excluded from ordinary operating working capital and not optimistically added to terminal equity.

## Failure definition and dividends

Minimum available cash is the greater of ₹1 crore and 0.05 month of operating cash costs; 0.25/0.5-month sensitivities test resilience. This small base reserve is an explicit policy assumption, not a lender covenant. A breach records the first month, reserve shortfall and maximum later shortfall. **A simulated breach is not an assertion that the company defaulted.** Every failed path retains all later counterfactual months and all contractual financing transitions. Failed paths receive no ordinary terminal valuation or return; negative bank balances in later diagnostic rows are unfinanceable residuals, not assumed overdrafts.

Annual dividends equal at most 10% of actual summed annual PAT and only available cash above reserve. Dividends leave bank cash and are divided by shares outstanding when paid. They are not also retained in terminal value. No future dividend is guaranteed by the ₹0.25 FY2026 declaration.

## Return, DCF and terminal consistency

The earnings cross-check uses terminal EBITDA × 7/10/12× for bear/base/bull, less all remaining debt, other claims and any specified senior/NCI claims, plus only cash above the required operating reserve. Negative equity is floored at zero. All shares issued in each path enter the terminal denominator. Entry and exit each incur 1% execution friction. IRR uses monthly-period date offsets from the November start (not day-exact XIRR from the 19 October quote), and solves annual dividends and terminal sale proceeds relative to that cost-inclusive entry, before personal taxes. Hurdle entry prices are separately computed at 12%, 15% and 18%; they are assumption-dependent, not executable bids or observed fair values.

FCFF is actual modeled collections plus operating advances less suppliers, operating costs, other operating-asset growth, operating cash tax and all capex. The increase in necessary cash reserve is deducted as additional operating reinvestment. The DCF discounts these monthly cash flows at a nominal INR WACC of 15% (12%/18% tested), separately estimated from the 15% investor hurdle. An illustrative analytical capital mix of 75% equity at 18% cost and 25% debt at 10% pretax cost, with 26% tax, gives 75% × 18% + 25% × 10% × (1 − 26%) = 15.35%, rounded to 15% for scenario work. This is not an observed capital structure, empirical beta estimate or measured market WACC; financing and tax-shield availability remain uncertain, so the range matters more than the point. It retains at least 36 months of post-horizon funding transition so new-credit maturities cannot disappear into a terminal value.

Normalized mature terminal FCFF subtracts replacement maintenance, incremental fixed assets, incremental operating working capital and cash reserve at the terminal-growth rate. Growth is at most the declared 3% (0–4% sensitivity), constrained by actual modeled growth, and is always below WACC. The full transition's discounted FCFF plus normalized terminal value is bridged to common equity with initial debt/claims, signed opening cash less reserve, actual conditional equity proceeds, and release of previously excluded/earmarked assets only when modeled. Negative opening reserve adjustment is not clipped into free cash. New equity proceeds and terminal diluted shares are both included; it is not free financing. Financing fees and crystallized claims are deducted separately. The reported terminal fraction shows reliance on distant value.

## Sensitivity, reverse expectations and reproducibility

Growth×exit-multiple, operating margin×WACC, WACC×terminal growth and collections×inventory grids retain complete cash paths, not just sensitivity cells. The purpose-built five/ten-year 5×/10× target grid is only a subset of all modeled paths, not an exhaustive crossing count: the separate ten-year main bull case also crosses 5× under its distinct 18% growth, 4.5% margin and 25/35/30-day working-capital assumptions. Five/ten-year 5×/10× tests distinguish no-new-financing from improved-conversion-plus-new-equity conditions. The grid is a bounded experiment, not a unique inverse solution or probability distribution. Required-return crossings are reported only among funded paths; a failed path cannot become attractive merely by raising its terminal multiple. Later reader tables show the least tested crossing growth where any exists and the assumptions that accompany it.

`run_model.py` generates all specs and complete compressed canonical paths deterministically. `model_audit.py` rebuilds every path exactly. `independent_replay.py` does not import the production engine and checks stock/flow recurrences, source buckets, debt principal/interest/tax, share issuance, capex, dividends, failures and terminal value on every raw row. Mutation tests deliberately corrupt cash, funding, shares, failure and valuation fields and require detection. Vintages are frozen before later changes; their original full path bytes stay on disk and source-safe compact reconstruction files reproduce their original hashes without PDFs or network. These are producer controls, not the separate independent acceptance review.
