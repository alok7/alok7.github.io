4. Business Overview

The legal listed entity makes/processes ground, blended and whole spices under SHYAM and distributes grocery/herbs/seasonings; MORVIK is described as an institutional/value-oriented brand in FY26. Facilities cover cleaning, grinding, blending, sorting and packing in Jaipur, with packaging/administrative functions also at VKI. These processes and quality systems can support repeat orders but are replicable. A strict manufacturing-versus-trading revenue bridge is ND: four product categories are reported, but classifying every grocery rupee as pure trade and every spice rupee as wholly in-house manufacture would exceed the evidence.
