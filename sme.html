How to read this collection

Screen: preliminary evidence-led work, not a completed forensic assessment. Deep: a completed applicable framework review, still subject to its stated limitations. Pending: research is not yet complete. Section count alone does not establish completeness.

BQS measures business quality; GQS measures growth quality; IAS measures investment attractiveness at a stated price. NS means insufficient support, not zero. Numeric scores require the v3.0 audit trail. A screen, a missing score or an unverified snapshot is never silently promoted to an investment conclusion.

Dates, financial scope and units are source-specific. Stale quotes are not live prices. Scores are research judgments, not statistically validated predictions. This is research, not personalized investment advice.
