A GUIDE TO THE SCORES

Three different questions.

01 / BQS

How good is the business?

Business quality weighs the operating franchise, governance, capital allocation, cash generation and balance-sheet resilience.

02 / GQS

How good is the growth?

Growth quality considers durability, execution and whether expansion can produce dependable per-share economic value.

03 / IAS

Does the entry price work?

Entry attractiveness is conditional on valuation, funding, downside and implementation assumptions in each company analysis.

Score definitions & research boundaries

Scores use the v3.0 framework and retain their original audit trail. NS means insufficient evidence, not zero. Only eligible scores enter a ranking; numerical assessments with evidence or gate limitations remain unranked. Equal displayed scores share a rank and are ordered alphabetically.

Reviewed means the applicable analysis is completed, not that every uncertainty is resolved or that the company qualifies for investment. Pending companies have not been published as completed reviews. The submitted SME universe is verified company by company; the earlier research archive also contains historical SME dossiers.

Research through 20 October 2026. Prices and sources are individually dated, not a live price feed. Scenarios are analytical assumptions, not promised returns. This is research, not personalized investment advice.
