03 — THE PROCESS

An idea earns
its place.

Our research framework connects the hypothesis to the portfolio, and the portfolio to the evidence. Attractive backtests are the beginning of the conversation.

  1. 01

    Define the hypothesis

    Start with an observable market behaviour and an explicit reason it might persist.

  2. 02

    Test in time order

    Use earlier observations to select rules, then evaluate subsequent portfolio periods.

  3. 03

    Challenge the result

    Inspect trades and drawdowns. Stress costs, entry timing and dependence on the largest winners.

  4. 04

    Validate execution

    Check instruments, fills, state and paper behaviour before considering deployment. Record what remains unresolved.

THE DETAILS MATTER

Read the fine print.
It’s part of the thesis.

Risk and evidence quality belong beside performance, not outside the frame.

What do these performance figures represent?+

Historical simulations, not live client returns, targets or independently audited results. The equity portfolios use daily marked-to-market equity. The archived strangle books profits and losses at expiry and uses index prices as an intraday futures proxy. Its Sharpe and drawdown are therefore based on a different, less complete accounting path.

CAGR uses the original studies’ 252-session annualisation. Sharpe deducts a 6.5% annual risk-free return. Cash earns no interest. Dates are shown for each strategy; no blended track record is presented.

What costs and constraints are included?+

VPT and Leader Volume were simulated at ₹10 lakh with 0.12% fees and 0.10% base slippage per side, plus the original volatility slippage and next-open limit rules. They retain marked open positions at the evaluation endpoint.

The archived strangle used ₹1 crore, dated trading charges, 0.5-point option slippage and modelled futures hedging costs. Intraday futures execution used a proxy and fractional hedge quantities. Management fees, performance fees and individual investor taxes are excluded. Larger capital can change liquidity, market impact and attainable returns.

Which research limitations remain?+

All three histories have been used during research; none is an untouched investment track record. Leader Volume improves the headline but underperforms VPT under combined doubled costs and delayed entries: Sharpe 1.1619 versus 1.2594. It has not been promoted.

The strangle’s execution/accounting fixes have not been validated through a complete multi-year native-contract backtest. Its archived result must not be treated as validation of the corrected implementation. Changes in market conditions can invalidate historical relationships.

What can go wrong?+

Equity momentum can suffer sharp reversals, prolonged drawdowns and liquidity constraints. The two equity variants overlap and should not be assumed to diversify one another.

The strangle sells options without protective option wings. A conditional futures hedge does not cap losses: gaps, volatility changes and margin calls can cause losses exceeding allocated capital. The archived 8.07% drawdown is not a loss limit.

Can I inspect the evidence?+

Download the performance summary, normalised chart data and source and methodology record. The latter identifies the archived run artifacts and their checksums. No private holdings, client records or broker credentials are included.
